Acme Technologies - Employee Leave Policy

1. Casual Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 casual leave days per calendar year.

Casual leave is intended for personal or urgent requirements.

Employees should normally apply for casual leave at least 2 working days before the leave start date.

Unused casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year.


2. Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 10 sick leave days per calendar year.

Sick leave can be used when an employee is unable to work due to illness.

For sick leave of more than 3 consecutive working days, the employee may be required to provide a medical certificate.


3. Earned Leave

Employees are entitled to 18 earned leave days per calendar year.

Earned leave should normally be requested at least 7 working days in advance.

Earned leave can be carried forward according to the company's annual carry-forward rules.


4. Leave Application

Employees must submit leave requests through the company's HR system.

Leave requests are subject to applicable leave balance and company approval rules.

A leave request cannot be submitted when the employee does not have sufficient leave balance.


5. Leave Cancellation

Employees can request cancellation of a previously submitted leave request.

A cancelled leave request restores the corresponding leave balance only after the cancellation is successfully processed by the HR system.


6. Manager Approval

Leave requests longer than 5 consecutive working days require manager approval.

The HR assistant cannot approve manager-required leave requests on behalf of a manager.


7. Holidays and Weekends

Company holidays and weekends are not counted as leave days when calculating working-day leave duration.
